# How Small Streamers Can Get Their First Sponsorship (Even Under 100 Viewers)

Most new streamers assume brand deals only happen once you hit thousands of followers.

That’s not true.

In fact, many brands today prefer working with smaller creators because they often have tighter communities, stronger engagement, and more authentic influence.

If you’re a growing streamer wondering whether sponsorships are possible for you, the answer is yes — but the path looks different than most people think.

---

## Why Follower Count Isn’t Everything

![Small streamer setup](/assets/blog-images/1-small-streamer-setup.jpg)

Brands don’t just buy exposure.  
They buy trust.

A streamer with 40 loyal viewers who chat, click links, and follow recommendations can be more valuable than someone with 5,000 passive followers.

What brands really look for includes:

- Consistent content schedule  
- A clear niche or theme  
- Audience interaction and chat engagement  
- Authentic personality and communication style  

If your viewers trust you, you already have something brands want.

---

## What Makes Small Creators Attractive to Sponsors

![Twitch streamer desk](/assets/blog-images/2-twitch-streaming-desk.jpg)

Micro-creators often convert better than larger influencers because their audiences feel more personal.

When your viewers see you as relatable rather than “famous,” recommendations feel genuine instead of promotional.

This makes smaller creators ideal for:

- Indie games  
- Small tech companies  
- New apps and software tools  
- Niche products targeting specific communities  

Many brands specifically look for creators under 10k followers for these campaigns.

---

## How to Position Yourself for Sponsorships Early

![Content creator streaming](/assets/blog-images/3-content-creator-streaming.jpg)

You don’t need to wait until you’re “big enough.”  
You need to look **ready enough**.

Here’s how:

### 1. Define Your Niche
Brands want to know who your audience is.

Instead of “I stream random games,” say:

- “I stream competitive FPS games for beginner players”
- “I focus on cozy simulation games”
- “I teach new streamers how to grow”

Clarity attracts partnerships.

### 2. Make Your Channel Look Professional
You don’t need fancy graphics, but you do need:

- a clear bio  
- consistent branding  
- links to social media  
- basic stream quality  

This signals reliability to potential sponsors.

### 3. Show Audience Interaction
Brands care about:

- chat activity  
- comments  
- returning viewers  
- community culture  

A small engaged audience beats a large silent one every time.

---

## The Biggest Mistake Small Streamers Make

![Micro streamer setup](/assets/blog-images/4-micro-streamer-setup.jpg)

Waiting too long to seek sponsorships.

Many creators assume they need:

- 10k followers  
- partner status  
- viral clips  

But brands often prefer to partner early with creators who are growing.

Getting sponsorship experience early can actually help you grow faster.

---

## The Reality of First Sponsorship Deals

Your first deal might not be huge.

It could be:

- a small paid promotion  
- a free product plus commission  
- affiliate partnerships  
- early-access collaborations  

But those first deals build your portfolio and credibility, making future opportunities easier to land.
