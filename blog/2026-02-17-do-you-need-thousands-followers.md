# Do You Need Thousands of Followers to Get Sponsored? (The Truth)

A lot of new streamers think you need 10k, 50k, or even 100k followers to get brand deals.

The truth? **Follower count isn’t everything.**  

Brands today are looking for engagement, niche audiences, and authenticity — qualities that small creators often have in spades.

---

## Why Micro-Creators Are Valuable to Brands

![Small streamer setup](/assets/blog-images/1-small-streamer-setup.jpg)

Smaller audiences mean tighter communities. Brands know:

- Your viewers are more likely to take action  
- You have higher trust with your audience  
- Your niche is often easier to target  

Many brands prefer creators under 10k followers for their campaigns. Smaller, loyal audiences often convert better than massive, passive ones.

---

## Engagement > Followers

Engagement metrics are more important than raw numbers.  

Brands evaluate:

- Chat activity  
- Social media interaction  
- Viewer retention  
- Comment quality  

Even with a small audience, if people actively watch and interact with your content, you’re already attractive to sponsors.

---

## How to Make Yourself Sponsorship-Ready Early

![Twitch streamer desk](/assets/blog-images/2-twitch-streaming-desk.jpg)

You don’t need to wait until you “hit big.” Focus on being ready:

1. **Polish your bio and branding** – Clear, professional, and niche-specific  
2. **Showcase your engagement** – Highlight loyal viewers and active community  
3. **Keep a consistent schedule** – Shows reliability to brands  
4. **Document your metrics** – Track chat, views, and growth trends  

---

## The First Deal Isn’t About Size

Your first sponsorship may be:

- A small paid promotion  
- Free products in exchange for coverage  
- Affiliate partnerships  
- Early access collaborations  

The goal is **experience** and **credibility**, not huge revenue. Every deal strengthens your portfolio and helps you land bigger opportunities later.

---

## Platforms Matter Too

Brands are platform-specific. Make sure you highlight your platform:

- **[Twitch](chatgpt://generic-entity?number=0)** – live streaming engagement  
- **[YouTube](chatgpt://generic-entity?number=1)** – replay and content reach

Different brands will value different platforms differently. Include this info in your application.
