# What Brands Actually Look for in Small Creators

If you’re a small streamer, it’s easy to assume brands only want massive audiences.  

The truth is: brands are looking for **engagement, authenticity, and niche audiences** — exactly what many small creators already have.

Understanding what brands want is the first step toward landing sponsorships early in your career.

---

## Key Factors Brands Evaluate

![Small streamer setup](/assets/blog-images/1-small-streamer-setup.jpg)

Brands pay attention to several key factors:

- **Audience engagement** – active viewers who participate in chat or comments  
- **Niche focus** – clearly defined content or audience  
- **Consistency** – reliable streaming schedule and presence  
- **Professionalism** – clean branding and easy-to-contact channels  
- **Growth potential** – small creators with upward momentum are very attractive  

---

## Engagement Metrics Over Follower Count

![Twitch streamer desk](/assets/blog-images/2-twitch-streaming-desk.jpg)

A creator with 50 engaged viewers who participate daily can be more valuable than one with 5,000 passive followers.  

Metrics brands often examine include:

- Average concurrent viewers  
- Chat activity  
- Likes, shares, and comments on social media  
- Viewer retention over time  

---

## How to Stand Out to Brands as a Small Creator

![Content creator streaming](/assets/blog-images/3-content-creator-streaming.jpg)

To attract sponsors:

1. **Polish your channel** – clear bio, consistent branding, and quality content  
2. **Showcase engagement** – highlight returning viewers and chat participation  
3. **Track metrics** – note your stats and growth trends  
4. **Present your niche clearly** – let brands know who your audience is  

These steps show brands that you are reliable, professional, and worth investing in.

---

## Micro-Creators Can Be the Most Valuable

![Micro streamer setup](/assets/blog-images/4-micro-streamer-setup.jpg)

Brands increasingly seek smaller creators because:

- They have dedicated audiences  
- Authentic recommendations convert better  
- They are easier to test campaigns with minimal risk  

Small creators who position themselves correctly can land sponsorships earlier than expected.
